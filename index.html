Profile

About

I am a master's student at Nagoya University, specialising in second language acquisition and language assessment.

My research focuses on how task repetition and different practice modalities influence second language oral fluency.

Current work

Research

My current research examines the effects of input, output, and combined input–output practice on the development, transfer, and retention of second language oral fluency.

L2 Oral Fluency

The development and measurement of speed, breakdown, and repair fluency.

Task Repetition

How repeated task performance supports proceduralisation and fluent speech production.

Language Assessment

Speaking assessment, performance measurement, and technology-assisted scoring.
